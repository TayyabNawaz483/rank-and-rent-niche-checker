How it Works & Logic Behind Evaluation

Rank and Rent Validation Logic

  • Keyword Difficulty (KD): Must be 10 or less to ensure rankings are beatable (KD ≤ 10).
  • City Zip Codes: City must have 2 or more zip codes to ensure it's large enough for a local business.
  • Search Volume: Must be at least 100. Volume below 100 is considered invalid.
  • Low DA Competitors: At least 4 to 5 sites in the top SERP must have a Domain Authority (DA) below 10.
  • GMB Reviews: All Map Pack GMB reviews must be 100 or less (Reviews ≤ 100). Single GMBs > 100 indicates high local dominance.

Technical Criteria

  • GMB Abundance: There must be at least **10 to 15 GMBs** active in the target area.
  • Directories Presence: At least one major directory (Yelp, YellowPages, etc.) must rank on page 1, proving search space availability.
  • Traffic Distribution: Target competitors must have traffic of at least 50 visitors/mo (Traffic ≥ 50).
  • Existing R&R / EMD / Micro Site: A Rank & Rent, EMD (Exact Match Domain), or Micro Site must already exist/be active in the SERP, confirming proof-of-concept.
